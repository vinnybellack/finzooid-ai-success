Data-driven customer success strategy using ChatGPT, dashboards, and automation to reduce churn and improve feature adoption.


🔹 AI-Powered Customer Success & Analytics – Finzooid (2019–2022)

This project demonstrates how I led the transformation of customer success operations at Finzooid using GenAI tools and data automation. As a Customer Relations Manager, I integrated generative AI into feedback analysis, reporting, and engagement workflows to improve retention, reduce churn, and drive proactive success strategies.
🤖 GenAI & Automation-First Approach

    ChatGPT + Internal Data → Summarized open-ended feedback into actionable themes within minutes

    AI-Powered Success Plans → Generated custom engagement content tailored to client usage trends

    Predictive Churn Dashboards → Used AI-generated signals to trigger timely success interventions

    Campaign-Linked Content → Matched LinkedIn/email posts with client segment behaviors

    📌 Replaced manual reporting and subjective feedback analysis with intelligent, repeatable GenAI workflows

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

    ChatGPT / GenAI – Feedback summarization, outreach messaging, campaign planning

    Power BI – Dashboards for CSAT, feature adoption, and churn risk analytics

    Buffer – Automated social scheduling aligned with lifecycle events

    SQL – Customer usage analysis & segmentation

    Google Sheets / Excel – KPI tracking and campaign calendar management

📈 Outcomes Delivered

    🚀 +30% feature adoption through AI-informed outreach triggers

    ⚠️ Churn risk flagged 2–3 weeks earlier using dashboard prediction models

    🧠 Reduced time-to-insight by 60% using GenAI-based summarization

    📣 Personalized content and success plans improved retention and executive engagement
